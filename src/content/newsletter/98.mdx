Hey everyone! I hope you've all had a fantastic couple of weeks. Welcome to another issue of the newsletter!

This week, we've got an important **CocoaPods deadline**, a tool for reclaiming disk space, and a look inside **Tuist's own rack of Mac minis**. There are two goodbyes to catch up on too: Lemon Squeezy is winding down, and Apple has closed its Support Community forums.

Let's dive in!

### [⏰ Reminder: CocoaPods trunk goes read-only on December 2](https://blog.cocoapods.org/CocoaPods-Specs-Repo/?utm_source=ioscinewsletter&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=issue_98)

A quick reminder: **next Wednesday, December 2**, CocoaPods trunk is scheduled to go **read-only**. Existing versions will remain available, but you won't be able to publish new pods or updates. If you've got a release lined up, check the team's timeline before the cutoff!

### [🧹 Reclaim disk space with Plonge](https://github.com/mattt/plonge?utm_source=ioscinewsletter&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=issue_98)

Between **DerivedData**, Swift Package builds, and agent worktrees, where did all your disk space go? [Mattt](https://x.com/mattt)'s **Plonge** finds disposable development caches and lets you preview a cleanup before deleting anything. It also comes with an **MCP server and agent skill**, so your coding agent can help tidy up after itself 👀.

### [🏗️ Tuist takes CI down to the metal](https://tuist.dev/blog/2026/11/04/runners?utm_source=ioscinewsletter&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=issue_98)

How close is your CI runner to its build cache? For **[Tuist](https://x.com/tuistdev) Runners**, they're in the same rack. The team shares why they started running their own **M5 Pro Mac minis in Berlin**, and what it took to get there, from networking to redundant power. A great peek behind the infrastructure, with details on how to **request an invite**.

### [🍋 Lemon Squeezy is winding down](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/blog/saying-goodbye-to-lemon-squeezy?utm_source=ioscinewsletter&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=issue_98)

If you sell through **Lemon Squeezy**, you'll need a new home for your payments before **March 31, 2027**. The team is focusing on **Stripe Managed Payments**, but it won't be a fit for every business. Their announcement explains the migration options, the feature gaps, and what to do if Managed Payments isn't available in your country.

### [🧪 Moving 10,000 tests to Swift Testing at Depop](https://engineering.depop.com/from-xctest-to-swift-testing-migrating-10-000-unit-tests-at-depop-4bc0b438eb12?utm_source=ioscinewsletter&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=issue_98)

How do you move **10,000 unit tests** to Swift Testing without drowning in review? [Anastasia Petrova](https://www.linkedin.com/in/anastasia-ios/) shares how Depop combined conversion scripts and AI to tackle the migration, speed up CI, and catch flaky tests. There are some great lessons here, including a scheme setting that could be holding your tests back 👀.

### [💬 Goodbye to Apple's Support Community forums](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1031507/apple-community-support-forum-shutting-down?utm_source=ioscinewsletter&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=issue_98)

After nearly three decades of users helping each other troubleshoot, **Apple's Support Community forums closed on November 20**. The Verge covers the goodbye, including the uncertainty around what would happen to all those old discussions. A sad loss of a long-running community.
