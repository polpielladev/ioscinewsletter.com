Hey everyone! I hope you've all had a great week. Welcome to another issue of the newsletter!

This time, we're starting with a look at how my good friend [Klemens Strasser](https://x.com/klemensstrasser), the developer behind **Art of Fauna**, plans and ships updates as a team of one. There's also news from Apple on **App Review** and **multiseat subscriptions**, plus two practical articles on finding simulators for CI and automating App Store screenshots.

Let's dive in!

### [🧩 Shipped with Helm: Art of Fauna](https://helm-app.com/changelog/shipped-with-helm-art-of-fauna/?utm_source=ioscinewsletter&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=issue_96)

For our latest **Shipped with Helm** interview, we spoke to [Klemens Strasser](https://x.com/klemensstrasser), the solo developer behind Art of Fauna and Art of Flora. He shares how he plans content updates around events such as Earth Day and Halloween, then submits **App Store feature nominations months ahead**, adding more detail as each release takes shape.

I especially like how simple his release setup is: a checklist, Xcode, Xcode Cloud, and Helm. The interview also covers accessibility audits, getting native speakers to review localizations, and his advice to ship earlier instead of adding feature after feature.

### [🚩 Feature flags don't bypass App Review](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=vvzx6cul&utm_source=ioscinewsletter&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=issue_96)

If you use **feature flags** in your release workflow, Apple's latest App Review update is worth a read. **Guideline 2.5.19** clarifies the prohibition on hiding functionality during review and enabling it after approval. For features behind a flag, the practical takeaway is to **describe them in your review notes and make them accessible to reviewers**. Flipping a remote flag after approval doesn't make undisclosed functionality exempt from review.

There are other updates too, including **user-generated content moderation** requirements that explicitly cover nonconsensual intimate imagery, including AI-generated content. Apps hosting this content must proactively prevent and remove it.

### [🏫 Offer your subscriptions to businesses and schools](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=yfo21auu&utm_source=ioscinewsletter&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=issue_96)

Organizations and schools can now buy **multiple seats for an auto-renewable subscription** through Apple Business or Apple School Manager. They make a single In-App Purchase, then assign and manage seats using their existing device management tools, adding or removing seats as needed.

Apple says **multiseat purchasing is enabled by default** in App Store Connect unless you've previously changed the setting, so it's worth checking your subscription's availability options. With **Helm 2.3.91**, you can check this setting in the app and **update it across all your subscriptions in bulk**.

Group purchases are coming too: sandbox testing starts in **November 2026**, with App Store availability planned for this winter.

### [🤖 Automate App Store screenshots with Claude Code](https://roland.leth.ro/blog/tech/automating-app-store-screenshots-with-claude-code?utm_source=ioscinewsletter&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=issue_96)

[Roland Leth](https://x.com/rolandleth) shares how he uses Claude Code to build and run an App Store screenshot exporter for his apps. Each screenshot is composed from **real SwiftUI components and controlled sample data** inside the debug build, then rendered directly into a PNG at the required size.

The article covers the details that make the workflow repeatable: fixed dates, separate fixtures, checks that stop invalid exports, and launch arguments that let an agent start the export. Really good read if you do this process manually today!
